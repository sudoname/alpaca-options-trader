## 4. Bottom line

1. **No tested strategy is profitable on this data.** The live gated system is
   the worst per-trade performer (−13.18% full-sample, −6.78% OOS); its 35.7%
   realized win rate is far below the ~58% breakeven its +24/−34 payoff
   asymmetry requires.
2. **The gates currently subtract value** relative to the decision stream they
   filter (negative gate edge in-sample and OOS), modulo the CF-exit caveat.
3. **The learners show filtering signal but no edge:** OOS Q-table ≈ breakeven
   on mark-derived P&L; logistic replay arm halves losses vs baseline. Neither
   clears zero.
4. Only bright spots: ORCL realized trades (+1.2%/trade) and OOS NVDA/TSLA
   (+15.1%/+9.6% on small n).

**Implication:** the selectivity thesis in `docs/ORACLE_PAPER.md` is not yet
supported by the paper record — selection is currently anti-selective. Before
arming further capital, the EV/profitability gates and exit rules (the −33.6%
average loss vs the design's capped-loss intent) deserve investigation ahead of
any new model training.
